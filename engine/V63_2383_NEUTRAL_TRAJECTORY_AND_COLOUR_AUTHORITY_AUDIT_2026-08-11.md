# V63: 2383 neutral trajectory and colour-authority audit

Date: 2026-08-11
Real-material check: `NJARAW_S001_S001_T020.MOV`, frame 0
Chart diagnostic: `NJARAW_S001_S001_T003.MOV`, frame 160
Release class: evidence-corrected projection-neutral coordinate

## Result

V63 corrects one coordinate error and deliberately leaves two underidentified
parts frozen.

The old final projected-gray table was generated by sending equal principal
2383 record densities through the lamp/print observer. After V58, however, the
three H-D curve coordinates are not equal: Kodak's `1.09 / 1.06 / 1.03`
Status-A LAD values are simultaneous integral measurements of one processed
patch, while the internal principal-density triplet solved from them is
approximately `0.98972 / 0.88206 / 0.84215 D`. Equal principal densities are
therefore no longer the gray trajectory produced by the active model.

V63 constructs the display-neutral table from the actual model trajectory:

```text
neutral scene exposure
  -> V61 joint ISO Status-M 5279 negative
  -> V62 spectral printer exposure
  -> V60/V58 principal 2383 H-D and LAD coordinate
  -> 2383 Callier + xenon spectral observer
  -> luminance-indexed neutral factors
```

No colour patch, vendor LUT, scan colour or creative grade is used to derive
that table. The off-neutral monitor colour authority remains the accepted
scan-referenced V31 boundary because the public evidence does not identify a
physical 5279-to-2383 colour transform.

## Why the old axis was wrong

Kodak H-61B specifies two different kinds of constraint:

1. a normally balanced LAD patch on 2383 has Status-A densities
   `1.09 R / 1.06 G / 1.03 B`; and
2. all six gray patches should appear neutral. Pink highlights with green
   shadows, or the reverse, indicate a contrast mismatch.

Those statements do not say that an arbitrary vector `[D, D, D]` in the
separated 2383 H-D coordinates is a processed neutral print. Earlier versions
implicitly made that substitution. V58 corrected the LAD point itself, but the
final observer table continued to use the obsolete equal-coordinate axis.

Primary source: Kodak, [LAD for KODAK VISION Color Print Film,
H-61B](https://www.kodak.com/content/products-brochures/Film/LAD-for-KODAK-VISION-Color-Print-Film-H-61b.pdf).

## Quantitative neutral-axis result

The audit evaluates 337 neutral scene exposures from -12 to +9 stops around
18% gray. Chroma is measured in OKLab over the usable non-clipped display
range.

| path | median chroma | P95 chroma | maximum chroma |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| V62 physical spectral observer | 0.0127148 | 0.0295409 | 0.0363699 |
| V63 physical spectral observer | 0.0000000269 | 0.0000002149 | 0.0000005066 |
| V62 delivered scan-referenced observer | 0.0002674 | 0.0029146 | 0.0045267 |
| V63 delivered scan-referenced observer | 0.0002676 | 0.0029146 | 0.0045268 |

The physical spectral gray error falls by roughly five orders of magnitude at
the median. The delivered branch changes much less because its low-frequency
colour is intentionally owned by the scan-referenced display adapter. This is
not evidence that the correction is unnecessary; it shows that an upstream
physical error had been mostly hidden by a downstream viewing policy.

## What V63 does not claim

### The density-domain neutral shaper is still underidentified

Before the projection observer, the engine also shapes the three raw 2383 H-D
curves toward a shared continuous neutral-density trajectory. Re-running that
stage shows maximum corrections of:

```text
red 0.11394 D
green 0.07213 D
blue 0.10396 D
```

This is a reasonable laboratory-style response to H-61's six-patch instruction,
but it is not measured from the six off-LAD Status-A triplets. V63 retains it
for continuity and labels it as an inference. Removing it would not be more
scientific; it would merely substitute raw graph-tracing crossover errors for
an unmeasured lab balance. The correct future replacement is a measured
neutral wedge, not another guessed curve.

### Ishii's 3x13 polynomial is not an extra interimage layer

Ado Ishii's 2003 EK5279-to-EK2383 experiment used more than 400 patches and a
three-output, 13-term polynomial, reaching Status-A RMS errors of about
`0.022 / 0.023 / 0.027 D`. That polynomial is an end-to-end transform from
negative Status-M densities to print Status-A densities. It jointly absorbs
negative dye spectra, printer exposure, positive-stock curves and chemical
interactions. Adding an invented 3x13 polynomial after our explicit spectral
chain would double-count those stages. The paper does not publish the EK5279
coefficients.

Source: A. Ishii, [Color Management Technology for Digital Film
Mastering](https://library.imaging.org/admin/apis/public/api/ist/website/downloadArticle/cic/11/1/art00055).

### Real interimage coefficients still require real separated exposures

The proper chemical experiment exposes one positive-film record through a
step scale while keeping the other records uniformly exposed, then converts
the resulting Status-A measurements to analytical dye density. That is the
measurement described in [US20020118211A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20020118211A1/en).
V62/V63 identity remains the explicitly unmeasured minimum-assumption endpoint;
it is not a claim that real 2383 has no interimage effect.

The negative's DIR coupling is already nonlinear and spatial: inhibitor
release, diffusion and receiver availability change density, local contrast,
colour purity and granularity before the print stage. Kodak's DIR literature
supports those classes of effect, but not our missing 2383 coefficients. See
[US6221569B1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US6221569B1/en).

## T003 colour-authority ablation

The outdoor T003 chart cannot establish absolute accuracy: its scene spectral
power distribution is unknown, and the DGK published Lab values do not state
the reference illuminant/observer in the supplied guide. Under an explicit
D50/Bradford diagnostic assumption, it is still useful as a rejection test.

| observer | primary median/max hue error | primary chroma ratio | natural median/max hue error | natural chroma ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| scan-referenced | 7.96° / 11.85° | 0.405 | 11.68° / 23.32° | 0.574 |
| physical spectral after neutral fix | 12.00° / 18.88° | 0.347 | 11.83° / 15.81° | 0.626 |

The physical branch improves the worst natural-patch error and natural chroma,
but worsens primary hue and chroma and slightly worsens the natural median.
That mixed result cannot justify promoting it as colour authority. V63 therefore
keeps the scan-referenced observer and records the physical path as a future
measurement candidate.

## Remaining measurements, in priority order

1. Process a calibrated neutral step wedge and colour target on 5279, contact
   print it to 2383 under documented printer lights/ECP-2D, and record Status-A
   plus spectral densities.
2. Measure the projected target under a documented xenon lamp, lens, screen and
   flare condition, preferably as spectral radiance or XYZ rather than a
   camera photograph.
3. Fit the existing physical stages jointly to those measurements; do not add
   a residual saturation operator merely because a reference frame looks
   richer.

Until those measurements exist, the scientifically accurate statement is:
V63 has a correct modeled neutral coordinate, a frozen provisional off-neutral
display authority, and an explicitly unidentified positive-film interimage
stage.
